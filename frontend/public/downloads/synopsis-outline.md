# Thesis Synopsis Outline

Adapt the headings and page limits to your university's prescribed format.

1. Title (clear, specific, includes population and design)
2. Introduction and background (1 page)
3. Review of literature (2-3 pages, recent and relevant)
4. Rationale / need for the study
5. Aim and objectives (primary and secondary)
6. Hypothesis (if applicable)
7. Materials and methods
   - Study design and setting
   - Study population, inclusion and exclusion criteria
   - Sample size with formula and source of inputs
   - Sampling technique
   - Data collection tools and procedure
   - Outcome measures
   - Statistical analysis plan
   - Ethical considerations and consent process
8. Expected outcomes and significance
9. References (Vancouver)
10. Annexures: consent form, patient information sheet, proforma, IEC approval letter
