# Thesis / Dissertation Chapter Checklist

Follow your university's prescribed order if it differs.

- [ ] Title page and certificates (guide, head of department, plagiarism, declaration)
- [ ] Acknowledgements
- [ ] Table of contents, list of tables, list of figures, abbreviations
- [ ] Abstract (structured: background, methods, results, conclusion, keywords)
- [ ] Introduction
- [ ] Aims and objectives
- [ ] Review of literature
- [ ] Materials and methods
- [ ] Observations and results (tables and figures, each referred to in the text)
- [ ] Discussion (compare each key finding with published work)
- [ ] Limitations
- [ ] Conclusion and recommendations
- [ ] Summary
- [ ] Bibliography (consistent Vancouver style)
- [ ] Annexures: proforma, consent forms, information sheet, master chart, IEC approval
